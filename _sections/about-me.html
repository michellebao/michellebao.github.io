I am certainly a researcher, technologist, organizer, and less certainly, an artist and writer, residing in the Bay Area. My research and technological work explores the complex and fraught impacts of sociotechnical apparatuses like AI. My art and writing explores anything and everything else. Things that I'm currently wondering about include good fortunes, the perils of context collapse, and if this website will be ingested by an AI model.

I received a B.S., Minor, and M.S. in Symbolic Systems, Ethics and Technology, and Computer Science, respectively, from Stanford University.
